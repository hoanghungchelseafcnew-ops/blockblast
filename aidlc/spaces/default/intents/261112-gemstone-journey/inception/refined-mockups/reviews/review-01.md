### Findings

| ID | Severity | Location | Finding | Required action | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-01 | Major | design-system-mapping.md > gem tokens (onGemPrimary, Amethyst white 4.10) | The Amethyst button text is white at 4.10:1, which is below the 4.5:1 limit in A2. The mapping justifies it as large text (20 w700). WCAG large text means at least 18.66px bold, and the mapping does not say whether 20 is dp or logical px. It also does not say what happens at textScale below 1.0, or whether every Amethyst surface (labels, links, chips) is large text. The same exception is not checked for Amethyst against the dark track. | Either pick a darker Amethyst or a dark onGemPrimary that reaches 4.5:1, or state in A2 that the button is the only Amethyst surface using the large-text exception and why it qualifies. Add the same check to the A2 row. | New |
| R-02 | Major | accessibility-checklist.md > A4 (progress bar) vs mockups.md > M-2 | Sapphire and Ruby fill has only 2.0–3.0:1 contrast against the dark track. A 1.5dp white border is added to fix this. The border is the only thing that meets the 3:1 requirement, so the bar's visibility rests on it. The mockup does not say whether the border is drawn over the blurred background plus 50% overlay, or what the bar looks like at 0% fill. The 3:1 claim for the border is also not backed by a measured ratio against the overlay. | Add the measured ratio of the border against the overlay and background. Add an acceptance criterion for 0% and for 100%. State that the numeric "2,850 / 4,200" and "%" labels carry the meaning if the bar fails. | New |
| R-03 | Major | mockups.md > M-4 Victory and interaction-spec.md > VictoryScreen interrupt | CONTINUE stops confetti, glow and sound immediately and locks after the first tap. Several cases are not covered. These are: a tap during the 180ms clear animation, a tap while the next-level image is still precaching, Back pressed while Victory is open, and app backgrounding during Victory. The spec says progress is saved before Victory opens, but does not say what a double-tap or the Back button does after the lock. | Add a short state table for Victory (celebrating, idle, leaving, final) that gives the result of CONTINUE, system Back, and backgrounding in each state. Give a testable criterion that Back during `leaving` does nothing. | New |
| R-04 | Major | mockups.md > M-1 Home State C and M-5a Journey Complete | The Journey Complete state shows PLAY with "Endless · Best 12,400". Endless mode itself (M-2e) is covered. The conditions for entering it are not stated, such as how Best is stored and what happens if the player quits endless with no Best. M-5a shows only BACK TO MENU, so the first transition into endless exists only through Home PLAY. The 6-gem row at 96dp is not specified for 360dp-wide phones. | Add the entry rule into endless, the first-time value of Best (for example 0 or a dash), and the layout at 360dp width for the 6-gem row. | New |

Long names like "Emerald · Sanding" in the header and "You will return to Amethyst · Rough Stone" in M-3 are not shown to wrap or shrink. With bundled Righteous at 1.3 scale, these may overflow on 360dp. The 48dp touch-target test in A1 does not cover the case where a larger text scale pushes buttons out of view. | Define wrap or shrink behavior for the header title and the M-3 return message. Add the longest gem and level name pair as a worked case in A12. Remove the ⚠️ by turning the assumption into a pass/fail threshold. | New |

The "Level N of 36" label is an added extra that no story requires. | Record in the handoff that tinted Icons.diamond is the shipped fallback until art exists. The lock after the unlock sound is not addressed when sound is muted (A10 says the result is still conveyed, but M-5 does not show how). | Say in M-5 what replaces the unlock sound when muted, for example the text "Amethyst Unlocked!" alone. | New |

### Summary

The set covers every story, screen, state and system case, and the user decisions are applied consistently. It is held back by five gaps that engineering would have to guess at: the Amethyst contrast exception, the progress-bar border proof, Victory interrupt behavior, the endless-mode entry rule, and the unresolved text-scale threshold. Fix those and it is READY. This pass is advisory, so Request Changes at the gate is how these become revisions.
